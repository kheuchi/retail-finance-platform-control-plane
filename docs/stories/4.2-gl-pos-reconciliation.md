## Context

> **TL;DR:** revenue in the books must come from real sales; manual journals are the classic way to fake it.

Anomaly A3 ([3.1](3.1-synthetic-accounting-data.md)) plants four manual revenue journals
in August 2026 with no sales behind them. A reconciliation that misses them is not a control.

## Acceptance criteria

> **TL;DR:** exact on clean data, loud on planted fraud.

- [ ] Compare GL revenue with POS net sales in EUR, per store and day, and per month.
- [ ] Difference within EUR 0.01 on every clean store-day (the books balance by construction).
- [ ] Exceptions list exactly the 4 A3 journals (EUR 3-12k, August 2026), matched against
      `landing/_ground_truth/`: 4 found, 0 missed, 0 false positives.
- [ ] Each exception shows journal ID, store, date, amount, and "no matching POS sales".
- [ ] An exception is a **business finding**, not a job failure. The job fails only if the
      reconciliation cannot run (missing data, zero rows).
- [ ] Test in CI with a small generated dataset that includes one fake journal.

## Design notes

- Manual journals are identifiable by source/type in `gl_journal`; the check must not rely on
  that alone, or a disguised journal would pass. Compare amounts, not labels.
