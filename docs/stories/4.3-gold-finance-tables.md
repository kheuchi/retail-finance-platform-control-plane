## Context

> **TL;DR:** Gold is the only layer anyone outside engineering reads, so it must be right and explainable.

Silver is clean but raw-grained (4.2m rows). Finance asks questions per store, month and
category. Gold answers them with a handful of tables, fixed definitions and column comments,
so an analyst or the AI agent cannot misread a number.

## Tables and definitions

> **TL;DR:** four tables, one definition per term.

| Table | Grain | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| `gold.daily_revenue` | store × channel × day | 21,221 |
| `gold.margin` | store × category × month | 5,880 |
| `gold.refunds` | store × cashier × month | 4,926 |
| `gold.budget_variance` | store × month of the budget year | 360 |

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Net sales | POS sales in EUR, excluding VAT, before refunds |
| Paid incl. VAT | What customers paid, after discounts (named to avoid "gross sales" confusion) |
| Gross margin | (net sales − cost of goods) / net sales |
| Returns | Refunds in EUR excluding VAT |
| Discount rate | discount / (paid incl. VAT + discount) |
| Budget to date | Budget pro-rated to the days with actuals, for revenue and cost |

## Tricky parts

> **TL;DR:** a partial month, a reshaped criterion, and an access check that would have blocked every run.

### 1. The partial month
- Data ends on 2026-09-25. Compared with a full-month budget, September looked like −16% for
  every store. Gold compares the month in progress with a pro-rated budget and flags it
  (`complete_month = false`).

### 2. The data corrected a criterion
- The plan said "S031 margin visibly drops from June". A probe before coding showed the discount
  rate rises from June (5.8% → 6.5%) but the margin only falls clearly from July. The criterion
  now says what the data shows.

### 3. The independent review, again
The reviewer (a fresh agent, story + diff only) found:

| Severity | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| High | Unity Catalog lists grants inherited from the catalog on each schema: the check would read the analysts' "USE CATALOG" as a leak and block Gold on every run | Fixed: judge each grant where it was made; also read table-level grants |
| Medium | Blind spots: `account users`, MODIFY on gold, USE SCHEMA not required | Fixed: allow-list for analysts and everyone-groups |
| Medium | Cost budget not pro-rated in the month in progress | Fixed |
| Medium | Store-months without budget silently dropped; budget table unchecked | Fixed: kept with `has_budget`, totals checked against Silver |
| Medium | Highlights printout could fail the job after the tables were written | Fixed: guarded |
| Low | Division by zero, misleading "gross" name, re-scanning Silver, weak tests | Fixed: `try_divide`, `paid_incl_vat_eur` + comments, one cached pass, sharper tests |
| — | Prove analysts' access with a real analyst login | Accepted: evidence is the live grants; there is no analyst user |

The live run confirmed finding 1 mattered: the grants printed exactly the shape the fix handles.

## Acceptance criteria
