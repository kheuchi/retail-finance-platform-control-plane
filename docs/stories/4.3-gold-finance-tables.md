## Context

> **TL;DR:** Gold is the only layer outside the engineering team is allowed to read.

## Acceptance criteria

> **TL;DR:** right totals, visible anomalies, traceable rows.

- [ ] 2025 net revenue EUR 38.9m (± 0.1%), gross margin 34-35%, returns ~1.4% of revenue.
- [ ] Store S031 margin visibly drops from June 2026 (A2).
- [ ] Store S017 refunds jump in Aug-Sep 2026, concentrated on cashier S017-C03 (A1).
- [ ] Budget variance = actual − budget, by store and month, for every month with actuals.
- [ ] A `finance-analysts` member can query Gold and **cannot** read Silver or Bronze.
- [ ] Unity Catalog lineage shows each Gold table's Silver sources ([4.4](4.4-quality-and-lineage.md)).
